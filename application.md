## MIMO detection

- The [4×4 802.11n receiver study](https://www.eurasip.org/Proceedings/Eusipco/Eusipco2007/Papers/c1l-a01.pdf) gives a detection budget of under 4 µs for a 16 µs interframe interval.
- [QuAMax](https://doi.org/10.1145/3341302.3342072) reports 10–20 µs of annealing for a target bit-error rate of 10⁻⁶ in its 48×48 BPSK experiment at 20 dB. This timing excludes programming and readout.
